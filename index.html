// DIRECTIVE — 001

"Obscurity is not security.
Obscurity is the precondition of security."

YOSCRYPT operates in the space between visible infrastructure and the substrate beneath it. We do not announce. We do not explain. We build tools for those who understand that every system contains a system within it — and that the second system is where the real work happens.


Cryptographic primitives are language. Protocols are grammar. We write in both, and we read everything the public channel was not meant to carry.

// DIRECTIVE — 002

"The most dangerous key is the one
no one knows is a key."

Trust is a vulnerability. Verification is the only protocol that does not expire. Every handshake is an attack surface. Every certificate is an assumption. YOSCRYPT builds for the assumption that the assumption is wrong.


We do not offer services. We do not have a roadmap. We have a discipline — and a set of open problems that have never been adequately solved. We are solving them, quietly, in the dark, the way serious work has always been done.
